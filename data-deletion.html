MIRA User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 8, 2026

If you want data associated with the MIRA Meta integration removed, you can revoke MIRA's Facebook or Instagram access in your Meta account settings and then disconnect the Meta integration inside MIRA.

Delete or revoke access

  1. Open Facebook or Meta Accounts Center and remove the MIra/Sentia business integration or revoke its permissions.
  2. Inside MIRA, open Settings → Connections → Facebook + Instagram and disconnect or remove the Meta connection.
  3. Delete any local Creator Studio drafts or generated media you no longer want retained.

Disconnecting the integration removes MIRA's ability to access the connected Meta account. Locally stored access tokens and connection metadata should be deleted when the integration is removed. If additional help is needed, contact the operator through MIRA's Facebook Page or Instagram professional account and request deletion of Meta integration data.
