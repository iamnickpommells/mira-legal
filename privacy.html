MIRA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

MIRA is an AI companion application operated for the account owner. This policy explains how the MIRA application uses information received through Meta platforms, including Facebook and Instagram.

Information MIRA may access

When the owner connects Facebook or Instagram, MIRA may receive account and business-asset information authorized through Meta, such as Facebook Page identifiers, Instagram professional-account identifiers, profile names, post metadata, comments, engagement information, and access tokens needed to perform approved actions.

How information is used

MIRA uses authorized Meta data only to provide features requested by the owner, including preparing and publishing approved posts, displaying connected-account status, showing comments and engagement, replying to comments, and moderating content when those permissions are enabled.

Storage and security

Meta credentials and tokens are stored in MIRA's private local application storage on the owner's authorized computer. MIRA does not sell Meta user data. MIRA does not use Meta data for third-party advertising or data brokerage.

Retention

Connected-account identifiers and tokens are retained only while the integration remains configured or until the owner disconnects the integration or deletes the associated local data. Social post drafts and local activity records may be retained for continuity and audit purposes until deleted by the owner.

Sharing

MIRA sends data to Meta only as required to perform authorized Facebook or Instagram actions. Generated media may also be sent to the image-generation provider selected by the owner when the owner uses that feature. Provider-specific privacy notices are shown inside MIRA before use.

Your choices

The owner can disconnect Meta access, delete local drafts, remove generated assets, and request deletion of locally stored Meta integration data. See the User Data Deletion Instructions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through MIRA's connected Facebook Page or Instagram professional account.
